How I work

Discovery before solutions

User story comes last. The problem, the constraint, and who it affects come first.

Trade-offs explicit, not buried

Every prioritization decision gets named before it gets committed — RICE, value/effort, or MoSCoW depending on the context.

Outcome over output

Features are inputs. What moved the metric after launch is the only scorecard that matters.
